🌲 Nofa Egypt
🔗 Website: https://nofaegypt.rowad.com/

Nofa Egypt traces its roots back to 1950, when it was founded by Saleh M. and his son Mohamed as a small wood flooring business in Downtown Alexandria. What began as a humble workshop evolved into a hallmark of craftsmanship and dedication to the art of wood flooring.

In 1953, Mohamed graduated from the School of Fine Arts, which significantly deepened the company’s artistic approach and specialization in wood flooring. Today, Nofa Egypt continues to combine tradition, expertise, and refined aesthetics in every piece it creates.
